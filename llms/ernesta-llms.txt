# Ernesta

> Ernesta is a direct-to-consumer custom-sized rug company. Founded by three Peloton co-founders (John Foley, Hisao Kushi, Yony Feng) and backed by True Ventures, Addition, and Platform Capital, it lets homeowners and interior-design professionals order high-design, made-to-measure rugs sized to the inch, sold online and through a growing set of showrooms plus a dedicated Trade Portal for designers.
